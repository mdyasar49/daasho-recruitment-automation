### 🎯 4. Role & Responsibilities: What will a BDE do?

As a **Business Development Executive (Field Sales)**, you are the face of Daasho on the ground in Chennai.

* **Merchant Acquisition:** Visit local restaurants, bakeries, cafes, supermarkets, and grocery stores across your assigned Chennai territory.
* **Pitching & Negotiation:** Explain the Daasho zero-commission advantage to business owners, store managers, and chefs.
* **Onboarding & KYC:** Collect basic business documents (FSSAI license, GST/PAN, bank details, menu card, food photos) and onboard them onto the Daasho Merchant App.
* **Menu Digitization & Setup:** Assist partners in uploading their menu/catalog and testing their first digital order.
* **Relationship Management:** Maintain warm, long-term relationships with merchants to ensure high order fulfillment and customer delight.
* **Territory Growth:** Own your assigned zone/pincode (e.g. Velachery, Anna Nagar, T. Nagar, OMR, Porur, etc.) and drive transaction density.
